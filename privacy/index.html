Privacy Policy

Effective 28 September 2026

In short
  • Your photos and designs never leave your iPhone.
  • There are no accounts, and we never ask for your name or email.
  • We collect anonymous usage statistics (for example “a template was exported”) to improve the app. They are not linked to you, not used for advertising, and never sold.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Photos and designs

Supa asks for access to your photo library so you can place photos into templates, and for permission to add photos so it can save your finished designs. Photos you pick are copied into Supa's private storage on your device to keep your drafts, and are deleted when you delete a draft. They are processed entirely on your device and are never uploaded to us or anyone else.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details. Supa checks with Apple, on your device, which collections your Apple ID owns.

Anonymous usage statistics

Supa sends a small set of anonymous events to understand how the app is used and where it can be better, for example: a screen was opened, a template was opened, photos were added, a design was exported or saved, a paywall was shown, a purchase was started or completed. Events include the template or collection involved, the app version, the app's language, and basic device information (such as the iOS version and device model).

Events are tied to a random identifier created when Supa is installed. It is not your Apple ID, advertising identifier or any other identifier from your device, and it is not connected to your name, email, photos or purchases. Deleting Supa removes it.

We do not use your IP address to determine your location, and we do not build a profile of you.

These statistics are processed by PostHog on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt), acting on our behalf, and are kept for up to 12 months.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of data with data brokers.
  • No collection of contacts, location, messages or photo content.

Children

Supa is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Because the statistics are anonymous, we cannot connect them to you. You can stop all data collection at any time by deleting the app. If you have questions or a privacy request, including under the GDPR, message us on Instagram at @wearesupa.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
